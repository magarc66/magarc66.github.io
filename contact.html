Contact Us

Got questions or comment for us at Chrono Studios?

  • Bugs or Technical Issues: If you encounter any bugs, glitches, or performance problems in our games, please let us know. Include as many details as you can such as screenshots, steps to reproduce the issue, and the device or platform you are using. This helps us resolve problems more quickly and improve the experience for everyone.
  • Career Opportunities: Chrono Studios is not hiring at this time, but we will be opening positions in the future as our team grows. When new roles become available, we will post updates here on the contact page and share announcements on our news page. We encourage those interested in joining our team to check back regularly for future opportunities.
  • Spam Concerns: If you receive an email from someone claiming to be Chrono Studios, please verify it carefully. We will never ask for personal information, payment details, or confidential data outside of official channels. Any messages you receive from us will only relate to support, updates, or inquiries you have personally submitted. If you are unsure about the legitimacy of a message, feel free to contact us directly through our official website.
  • Video, Music Usage, Streaming Guidelines: You're welcome to steam or make videos and monetize them if you want. You're welcome to use our music as part of such content. It's called free advertisement!
  • Emulation: We understand that some players enjoy experiencing games through emulation, and we are comfortable with fans playing our titles this way as long as it is done responsibly. Please ensure that you own a legal copy of the game before using any emulator, and avoid downloading or sharing unauthorized versions of our work. We also do not support or endorse modified game files, cheats, or content that could harm the experience of others. If you choose to emulate our games, we kindly ask that you do so in a way that respects the hard work of our team.
