The Chrono Studios Team

Miguel

Lead Game Designer

A passionate storyteller who believes that gameplay should always serve a deeper emotional purpose, our Lead Game Designer brings heart and vision to every project. Their design philosophy focuses on building experiences that stay with players long after the game ends, blending narrative depth with mechanics that feel intuitive, rewarding, and alive. They served as the lead designer behind the combat system of Kingdom of the Maverick Knights and help crafting the emotional story arcs in our next upcoming RPG project, Project Chrono. Their leadership style is warm, collaborative, and driven by curiosity, always encouraging the team to push boundaries and explore bold ideas. Known for creative strengths in world building and systems design, they work tirelessly to shape adventures that inspire players and create lasting memories.

Mirnes

Art Director

Our Art Director is a dedicated visual storyteller who specializes in pixel art, unique visual identity, and imaginative world building. Their passion for classic game artistry began with the magic of the SNES era, where vibrant colors, expressive sprites, and handcrafted detail shaped the way they see games today. They draw deep inspiration from the modern HD-2D style, blending retro charm with contemporary depth to create worlds that feel nostalgic yet fresh. Known for their warm creative spirit and attention to small visual moments, they strive to make every environment and character feel alive. Their work guides the team in shaping experiences that are both beautiful to look at and rich in atmosphere, inviting players to step into worlds filled with heart and wonder.

Samir

Lead Programmer

Our Lead Programmer is the architect behind the systems that bring our games to life. With a deep focus on gameplay mechanics and player centered design, they ensure that every action feels responsive, intuitive, and meaningful. They believe that great programming is invisible, revealed only through the smooth experience that players feel moment to moment. Known for their problem solving mindset and love of experimentation, they work closely with designers and artists to turn creative ideas into fully realized interactive experiences. Their dedication to clean systems and polished functionality helps shape our games into adventures that feel seamless, engaging, and full of personality.
