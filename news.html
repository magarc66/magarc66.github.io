Thank You and Our Next RPG Project

We want to extend our heartfelt gratitude to every player who joined us on the journey of Kingdom of the Maverick Knights. Your support, feedback, and enthusiasm have helped the game grow into a critical and community success, and it means the world to our team. Because of this incredible response, we are excited to share that development has already begun on our next RPG project, currently titled Project Chrono. While it is still early in its creation, our goal is to push our storytelling, world building, and gameplay even further. We look forward to sharing more details as this new adventure takes shape. For the bottom of our hearts, thank you so much!

Chrono Studios' First Indie Video Game Release Today

Chrono Studios proudly announces the official release of its very first indie video game, marking an exciting milestone for the newly launched studio. This debut title represents a decade of dedication, creativity, and persistence — a passion project the team has worked on for 10 years before officially forming the company. The team expressed how grateful and excited we are to finally bring our long awaited project into the hands of players everywhere. With this launch, Chrono Studios is stepping confidently into the indie scene and looking ahead to future innovations.

Chrono Studios is Born

Today, we are excited to announce the launch of Chrono Studios. We are an independent game studio focused on building role playing experiences that honor the warmth of classic games while exploring new storytelling possibilities. Our team brings together designers, artists, and programmers who share a passion for rich worlds, memorable characters, and gameplay that matters. Over the coming months we will share dev updates, art, design insights, and playable demos as our projects move from prototypes to full games. We invite fans and creators to follow us, offer feedback, and join the community as we grow. Thank you for being here at the start of this adventure.
